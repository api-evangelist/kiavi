The Burns + Kiavi Fix and Flip Housing Market Index (FFMI)™ fell to 59 in the second quarter of 2026, down from 63 in the first quarter, according to the quarterly John Burns Research and Consulting and Kiavi Fix and Flip Survey. All three subindices declined as the 30-year fixed mortgage rate climbed through the quarter, reaching 6.69% by early August. Readings above 50 still indicate market expansion, and the index sits above the 57 recorded one year earlier, though slower exits and softer pricing power may warrant more conservative after-repair value assumptions.
