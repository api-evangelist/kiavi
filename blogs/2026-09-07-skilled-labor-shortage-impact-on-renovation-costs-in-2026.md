Skilled trades are in stronger demand than they've been in years, and that demand may be showing up in both paychecks and renovation budgets in 2026. More than 80% of contractors report competing hard for craft workers, and 40% raised base pay more in 2025 than the year before, according to the Associated General Contractors of America. For real estate investors, that strength in the trades workforce could be translating into higher renovation costs and longer contractor lead times that may be worth planning around.
