Financing a 2-4 unit property typically comes down to two paths: a short-term bridge loan for acquisition and renovation, or a longer-term DSCR loan sized to the property's combined rental income. New construction of 2-4 unit buildings made up just 3% of multifamily starts in the second quarter of 2026, according to the National Association of Home Builders , which could keep demand concentrated on the existing small multifamily stock. Choosing between the two loan types often depends on the property's condition at purchase and how quickly it could generate qualifying rental income.
