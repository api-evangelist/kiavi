The best time to sell a house, including one you've just finished flipping, typically falls in spring, when seller premiums peak at 10.7% in March and 10.2% in April and May, according to ATTOM's 2026 analysis of more than 52 million home sales. But the Q2 2026 JBREC + Kiavi Fix-and-Flip Survey found that 59% of real estate investors still reported longer days on market this spring, as a 40-50 basis point jump in mortgage rates offset the usual seasonal tailwind.
